System Guide

What every section is for, what is on it, and why the company needs it.

Covers the payroll and workforce system at jeyancowebsite-production.up.railway.app
Prepared 8 September 2026

Paano basahin ito. Ingles ang bawat pindutan at pamagat sa screen, kaya Ingles din ang paglalarawan dito — para tugma sa nakikita mo. Ang Bakit kailangan sa dulo ng bawat section ay nasa Tagalog: iyon ang bahaging tungkol sa kompanya, hindi sa software.

Part 1 — The shell

The frame around every page. Learn this once and every section behaves the same way.

1.1

The rail on the left

Every section of the system is one item on the navy rail. It is grouped: the daily work at the top, people below it, then pay, then the system's own settings at the bottom. The item you are on is the one filled in blue.

Bakit kailangan: Isang lugar lang ang pasukan sa lahat. Walang hahanaping link at walang matatandaang address — nasa rail ang lahat, laging nasa parehong pwesto.
1.2

The top bar

What is on it
  • Search data — searches records across the system.
  • ? — opens a short guide to the section you are on: what it is for, what the main buttons do, and the one thing people get wrong. It is the short version of this document.
  • Sun / moon — light or dark screen. This is your own choice and is remembered in your browser; it beats the company default.
  • Bell — notifications.
  • Your name and role — the letter beside it is drawn by the system, so it is there even with no connection.
Bakit kailangan: Ang ? ang tulong na hindi kailangang hanapin. Bagong tao sa opisina? Hindi mo na kailangang tabihan — nasa page mismo ang paliwanag.

Part 2 — Daily work

What somebody opens on an ordinary day.

2.1

Dashboard

/dashboard
What is on it
  • Tiles for today: Active Workers, Present Today, Still Timed In, This Week Payout, Outstanding Vale.
  • Four shortcuts: Register Worker, Payroll Run, Attendance, Payroll Records.
How it works
  • Nothing is entered here. Every figure is read from another section.
  • A number that looks wrong is corrected where it was recorded, not here.
Bakit kailangan: Sagot sa tanong na "kumusta ngayon?" nang hindi binubuksan ang lima-limang page. Ilan ang pumasok, sino ang naka-time in pa, magkano na ang gastos ngayong linggo — isang tingin lang.
2.2

Attendance

/attendance
What is on it
  • Today's Attendance — the current day's time in and time out.
  • History — the days before it.
  • Mark for Deletion, then Delete Selected or Delete All.
How it works
  • Mark for Deletion turns on the checkboxes. Delete Selected removes only what you ticked.
  • Attendance is what payroll counts. Correct it before a payroll run, not after.
Bakit kailangan: Ito ang pinagmumulan ng sahod. Bawat oras na mali dito ay maling bayad sa dulo — at mas mahirap ayusin kapag naibigay na ang pera.
2.3

Site Attendance

/site-attendance
What is on it
  • Attendance taken at a site rather than the office, listed a day at a time.
  • Counts above the list: records for the day, how many are still timed in, overtime hours.
  • Apply narrows by the filters; Today snaps back to the current day.
Bakit kailangan: Hindi lahat ng manggagawa ay dumadaan sa opisina. Kung nasa site sila kinukuha ang oras, dapat may sariling talaan yun na tugma pa rin sa payroll.

Part 3 — People

Who works for the company, and what the company owes them.

3.1

Employee Directory

/employees
What is on it
  • Every employee, as a table or as cards — the two icons on the right of the toolbar switch between them, and your choice is remembered.
  • Tabs: All Employees, Regular, Contractual.
  • Per row: site, labor type, shift, rate, vale balance, fingerprint count.
How it works
  • Add Employee creates the record. Export downloads the list as you are looking at it, filters included.
  • View Details opens the full record. Editing starts there on purpose — you see the whole record before you change part of it.
  • The three-dot menu on each row: Set Vale (adjust the balance by hand), Copy ID (the badge number, for reading to payroll), Delete.
  • The Shift dropdown saves as soon as you change it — a tick appears beside it.
Bakit kailangan: Dito nakasalalay ang lahat. Ang rate, ang site, ang uri ng trabaho — ito ang binabasa ng payroll. Isang maling rate dito ay maling sahod para sa bawat panahon hanggang may makapansin.
3.2

Leave and Overtime

/leave-overtime
How it works
  • File Leave and File Overtime record a request. It stays pending until somebody acts on it.
  • Approve or Reject decides it, and payroll reads that decision.
  • Apply filters the two lists; Reset clears the filters.
Bakit kailangan: Ang overtime at leave ay pera. Kapag pasalita lang, walang matitirang patunay kung sino ang nag-apruba. Dito, may pangalan at may petsa.
3.3

Loans and Advances (Vale)

/loans
How it works
  • New Loan / Advance records what was handed over. The balance starts at the full amount.
  • Payroll collects against it each period, so the balance falls on its own.
  • Record payment is for anything paid back outside a payroll run.
Bakit kailangan: Karaniwan ang vale sa konstruksyon. Kapag nasa papel o nasa isip lang, may nakakalimutan — at ang nakakalimutan ay palaging kompanya ang lugi.
3.4

Sites

/sites
How it works
  • Add Site creates one. The name is what shows everywhere a site is chosen.
  • Removing a site reassigns its employees automatically — nobody is left without one.
Bakit kailangan: Nagbabago ang proyekto. Kailangang malaman kung sino ang nasaan, at hindi dapat mawalan ng site ang tao kapag natapos ang isang proyekto.
3.5

Project Assignment

/project-assignments
How it works
  • New Assignment puts an employee on a project for a period.
  • End closes an assignment without deleting it, so the roster keeps its history.
Bakit kailangan: Para masagot ang "sino ang nasa proyektong ito noong Agosto?" — kahit tapos na ang proyekto at ibang site na ang tao ngayon.

Part 4 — Pay

The order matters here. Each step feeds the next one.

4.1

Payroll Processing

/payroll-processing
How it works
  • New Payroll Run picks the period. Create & Calculate works the figures out straight away.
  • Nothing is paid until you finalise, so Review is where you check first.
  • Approving a run is what makes its payslips appear in Payslips.
Bakit kailangan: Ito ang pagitan ng "kinuwenta" at "binayaran". Ang hakbang na ito ang nagbibigay ng pagkakataong mahuli ang mali bago pa lumabas ang pera.
4.2

Payroll Records

/payroll-records
What is on it
  • A breakdown of pay for the period chosen above the table.
  • Summary bar: Net Payroll, Gross Pay, Deductions, Overtime, Holiday Pay, Rest Day Pay, Bonus, Employees, Hours / Days.
How it works
  • Weekly (Mon–Sun) and Daily change how the period is grouped.
  • Preview & Download opens the printable copy; Print or Save as PDF from there.
  • "No records for this period" means exactly that — pick another week, or the run has not been made yet.
Bakit kailangan: Ito ang isasagot sa BIR, sa auditor, at sa manggagawang nagtatanong kung bakit ganoon ang sahod niya noong nakaraang linggo.
4.3

Payslips

/payslips
How it works
  • Payslips appear here once their payroll run has been approved in Payroll Processing.
  • View opens one. Print All prints the whole filtered list in a single pass.
  • A missing payslip means its run has not been approved yet.
Bakit kailangan: Karapatan ng manggagawa ang payslip. At kapag may tanong sa kaltas, ang papel ang sumasagot — hindi ang alaala.
4.4

Payroll Reports

/payroll-reports
How it works
  • Pick the report and the period, then Apply. Print produces the copy to hand over or file. Reset returns to the default period.
Bakit kailangan: Ang buod na hinihingi ng may-ari at ng gobyerno — hindi ang bawat linya, kundi ang kabuuan.
4.5

Payroll Settings

/settings
What is on it
  • Tabs: Multipliers and Deductions, Attendance, Labor Types, Holidays, Bonus & Vale.
How it works
  • Save changes applies from the next payroll run. A period already paid is not rewritten — rates are kept as history, not overwritten.
  • Add Labor Type creates a new rate that employees can then be put on.
Bakit kailangan: Dito nakasulat ang patakaran ng kompanya sa sahod — holiday pay, rest day, kaltas. Kapag nagbago ang batas o ang patakaran, isang lugar lang ang babaguhin, hindi bawat kuwenta.

Part 5 — Oversight

Reading, checking, and answering for what happened.

5.1

Analytics and Insights

/analytics
  • The charts read the same records as the rest of the system. Nothing is entered here.
  • Figures follow the period and filters chosen on the page.
Bakit kailangan: May mga bagay na hindi mo makikita sa isang linggo pero halata sa tatlong buwan — tumataas na absent, lumalaking overtime, sitwasyon kada site.
5.2

Jeyanco AI

/ai-assistant
  • Ask in plain language about payroll, attendance and the workforce.
  • Prompts holds ready-made questions. New Chat clears the thread.
Bakit kailangan: Hindi lahat ay marunong maghanap sa report. Ang tanong na "sino ang pinakamaraming overtime nitong buwan?" ay dapat masagot nang hindi natututo ng bagong screen.
5.3

Device Monitoring

/device-monitoring
  • The devices that report in, and when each was last heard from. Nothing is edited here.
Bakit kailangan: Kung tumigil ang fingerprint scanner sa isang site, hindi ka dapat makaalam nito sa araw ng payroll. Dito mo makikita agad.
5.4

Audit Logs

/audit-logs
  • A record of what changed, who changed it, and when.
  • Entries cannot be edited. That is the point of a log.
Bakit kailangan: Kapag may nagbago sa rate o may nabura sa attendance, may sagot sa "sino at kailan". Proteksyon ito sa kompanya at sa empleyado nang sabay.

Part 6 — System Settings

Four tabs down the left of one page. Admin work, not daily work.

6.1

Company

/system-settings
  • Name, tagline, address and logo. These appear on payslips, exports, the sidebar and the sign-in page.
  • Save writes at once, and the rest of the system follows on the next page load.
Bakit kailangan: Ang pangalan sa payslip ay dokumento. Kapag nagbago ang address o logo ng kompanya, isang beses lang binabago — hindi sa bawat template.
6.2

Accounts & roles

/accounts
  • Create Account makes a login and gives it a role.
  • On each row: the pencil edits, the person icon deactivates without deleting, the bin removes the account for good.
  • Your own row has only the pencil — you cannot deactivate or delete the account you are signed in with.
Bakit kailangan: Kapag umalis ang isang tao, i-deactivate — huwag burahin. Mananatili ang kasaysayan ng ginawa niya, pero hindi na siya makakapasok.
6.3

Appearance

/system-settings/appearance
  • Default theme — what a screen opens on the first time it is used. Anyone who uses the top-bar toggle keeps their own choice, and that beats this one.
  • Language — English or Tagalog for the system's own labels.
Bakit kailangan: Ang bagong telepono, bagong browser, o kiosk sa site ay bubukas sa pinili ng kompanya — hindi sa random.
6.4

Security

/system-settings/security
  • Session timeout, minimum password length, maximum login attempts, lockout length.
  • These are enforced by the system — the login page and the account forms both read them.
Bakit kailangan: Sahod at personal na datos ang laman nito. Ang naiwang bukas na screen sa site office ay panganib, at ang session timeout ang sumasara niyan.

Part 7 — One full pay cycle

The order the sections are meant to be used in. Skipping a step is where most mistakes come from.

#SectionWhat happens
1EmployeesEveryone who will be paid exists, with the right rate, site and labor type.
2Attendance / Site AttendanceHours are in for the whole period, and corrected while they can still be corrected.
3Leave & OvertimeEvery request for the period is approved or rejected. Nothing left pending.
4Loans & AdvancesAny vale handed out during the period is recorded, so payroll can collect against it.
5Payroll SettingsChecked, not changed — unless a rule genuinely changed. Changes apply to the next run.
6Payroll ProcessingNew Payroll Run → Create & Calculate → Review → approve.
7PayslipsPayslips appear. Print all, hand out.
8Payroll Records / ReportsFile the period's breakdown and the summary.
Ang isang tuntunin na nagpapaliwanag ng marami: ang mga bagay na naitala na ay hindi binabago nang paatras. Ang pagpapalit ng rate ngayon ay hindi nagbabago ng sahod na binayaran na noong nakaraang buwan, at ang audit log ay hindi mabubura. Ito ang dahilan kung bakit bago mag-approve ang oras ng pag-check, hindi pagkatapos.
